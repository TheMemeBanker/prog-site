Fees, programmed.

Every coin launched on OTC carries a program: what it pays its holders, and in which asset. Its PumpAMM creator fees are claimed about once a minute and the program runs — most of every fee buys the reward asset and goes out to holders pro-rata, the rest feeds the desks, the burn and the protocol. Nothing to claim, nothing to sign. This is the desk where you watch it run.

      What a smart launch programs

      The launcher writes the program at launch: the reward asset — a tokenised stock, a basket of up to ten, or another token — and the split below. The deployer can update a basket's lineup at any time. The fees do the rest.
